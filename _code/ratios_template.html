• Gross profit margin = gross profit ÷ revenue
  • Net profit margin = net income ÷ revenue
  • Current ratio = current assets ÷ current liabilities, at quarter end
  • OCF = net cash from operating activities
  • Free cash flow (FCF) = OCF − capital expenditures
  • Columns = calendar quarters; period ends are rounded to the nearest month end
  • * on a quarter = revenue and net income derived as annual (10-K) minus nine-month year-to-date
  • Cash flows are filed year-to-date, so every quarter's OCF and capex = YTD minus the prior YTD
  • — = not filed; airlines, cable, and some others report no gross-profit line
