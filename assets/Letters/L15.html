Step 1: Collect Premiums First

EX. An insurer collects $100 of premiums in advance for one year of coverage.

Jan 1:Cash (+A)100
Unearned Premiums (+L)100
  • Like deferred revenue (Ch10): cash now, revenue as coverage is provided.

Step 2: Earn the Premium, Record Losses
Dec 31:Unearned Premiums (−L)100
Premium Revenue (+R)100
Dec 31:Loss Expense (+E)95
Loss Reserve (+L)95
  • Claims of $95 are incurred but not yet paid → loss reserve (an estimate).

Step 3: Underwriting Profit and Float
Amount
Premium revenue$100
− Losses and expenses(95)
Underwriting profit$5
  • Float: cash held until claims are paid; the insurer invests it meanwhile.
  • Underwriting profit → float costs nothing to use.
