What the Letter Says

“We have a cash generative operating cycle because we turn our inventory quickly, collecting payments from our customers before payments are due to suppliers.”

Jeffrey P. Bezos, 2004 letter

Step 1: Days in Inventory
  • Inventory turnover = CGS ÷ average inventory.
  • Days in inventory = 365 ÷ inventory turnover.

EX. Amazon (2002): inventory turns 19 times a year → 365 ÷ 19 ≈ 19 days.

Questions
