  1. Retained earnings grew from 89,814 (Jan 31, 2024) to 98,313. Increase =P   Net income attributable to Walmart (F) was larger than this increase. Why?
    Retained earnings = beginning + net income − dividends (Walmart also charges part of its share buybacks here). It earned 19,436, but most of the difference was paid out to shareholders.
  2. Accounts payable (58,666) is larger than inventories (56,435), and current liabilities exceed current assets. What does that tell you about Walmart?
    Walmart sells the goods (about every 40 days) before it has to pay suppliers, so suppliers are financing its inventory. For Walmart this reflects bargaining power, not trouble.
  3. LIFO. Walmart’s U.S. stores use LIFO. When costs are rising, LIFO vs. FIFO gives cost of sales higher/lower, net income higher/lower, ending inventory higher/lower.
