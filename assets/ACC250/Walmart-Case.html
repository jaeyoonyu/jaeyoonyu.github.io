How the accounts connect

  1. Gross profit = Net sales − Cost of sales I    Gross margin % = Gross profit ÷ Net sales J
  2. Purchases. Inventories were 54,892 a year earlier. Beginning inventory + Purchases − Cost of sales = Ending inventory.
    How much inventory did Walmart buy this year? K
  3. Retained earnings were 89,814 a year earlier. By how much did they increase? L
    Walmart earned 19,436, so why is the increase smaller?
    Part of the profit went back to shareholders as dividends (and share buybacks), which reduce retained earnings.
  4. Accounts payable (58,666) is larger than inventories (56,435). What does that tell you?
    Walmart sells its goods before it has to pay its suppliers, so suppliers are financing its inventory.
