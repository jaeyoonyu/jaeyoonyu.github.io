From the 10-K (accounting policy)“Property, equipment, furniture, and fixtures are stated at original cost, less accumulated depreciation. Depreciation is provided using the straight-line method over the estimated useful asset lives. The useful lives … typically range from 2 to 10 years for machinery and equipment and furniture, fixtures and others.”

Accounts & Terms

Property and equipment (PPE), at cost
What the company paid to buy the assets and get them ready to use (cost principle). An asset.
Accumulated depreciation (AccDep)
The total depreciation recorded so far on the assets still owned. A contra-asset subtracted from PPE.
PPE, net (book value)
PPE at cost − AccDep: the cost not yet depreciated. It is not the market value.
Depreciation expense
This year’s share of the cost. Straight-line: (Cost − Residual value) ÷ Useful life.
