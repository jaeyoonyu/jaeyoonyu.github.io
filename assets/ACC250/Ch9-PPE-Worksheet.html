From the note: “We record property and equipment at cost and depreciate … on a straight-line basis to their estimated residual values over their estimated useful lives.” Residual values for owned aircraft and engines are generally 5% to 10% of cost.

Accounts & Terms

Property and equipment (PPE), at cost
What the company paid to buy the assets and get them ready to use (cost principle). An asset.
Accumulated depreciation (AccDep)
The total depreciation recorded so far on the assets still owned. A contra-asset subtracted from PPE.
PPE, net (book value)
PPE at cost − AccDep: the cost not yet depreciated. It is not the market value.
Depreciation expense
This year’s share of the cost, on the income statement. Straight-line: (Cost − Residual value) ÷ Useful life.
