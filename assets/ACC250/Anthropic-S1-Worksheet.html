BackgroundAn S-1 is the document a company files with the SEC before it sells stock to the public for the first time (an IPO). It contains audited financial statements, risk factors, and management’s discussion of the numbers. Anthropic makes Claude and earns revenue from API use and subscriptions.

Hints

Revenue recognition
Record revenue when it is earned. Cash received before the service is delivered is unearned revenue, a liability.
Convertible notes
Loans that the lender can turn into the company’s stock. Anthropic reports them at fair value: when the stock becomes worth more, the liability grows and a loss is recorded.
Liability
An obligation arising from a past event. A promise to buy services in the future is a commitment, disclosed in the notes.
Stock-based compensation
Paying employees with shares: Dr Compensation expense, Cr Additional paid-in capital. An expense, but no cash leaves.
Non-GAAP (“adjusted”) measures
Management’s own profit measures. They usually leave out stock compensation and one-time or non-cash items.
Accrual vs. cash
Net income records events when they happen; the cash flow statement shows the cash. Non-cash losses reduce net income but not cash.
