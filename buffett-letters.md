Warren Buffett&rsquo;s annual letters to Berkshire Hathaway shareholders ([berkshirehathaway.com/letters](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/letters.html), 1977&ndash;2024) explain many accounting issues in plain English. The table lists the main themes, the letters that discuss them most, and where they fit in ACC250 (Intro to Financial Accounting) and ACC531 (Auditing). Each theme has a one-page question sheet with an answer key.
